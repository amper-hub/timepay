# TimePay Philippine Payroll Rules Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-28
**Scope:** Current working-tree implementation on `chore/dependency-security-audit`; documentation and source review only.
**Decision:** Not ready for real payroll. Keep final approval disabled for statutory payroll until the blockers in this audit are resolved and reviewed by a qualified Philippine payroll/accounting professional.

This audit describes implementation behavior, not legal advice. "Confirmed" below means the cited primary government material states the rule as described. It does not establish that a rule applies to every employee or employer using TimePay. Legal applicability depends on worker classification, establishment size/type, region, schedules, benefit facts, and the applicable issuance for the actual payroll date.

## Implementation and approval overview

- Payroll profile schema has salary type, pay frequency, eligibility flags, `overtime_eligible`, `rank_and_file`, standard hours/day and standard workdays/month. Compensation history has hourly/daily/monthly basis, rate and effective dates. Profile items have a fixed amount, a taxable boolean, authorization reference and effective dates.
- There are no persisted employee tax class/MWE/region, covered-member status, work schedule/rest day, time-break, overtime approval/hours, holiday calendar, leave balance, employment start/separation reason/date, final-pay components, accumulated benefit/exemption, prior-employer tax/benefit, or statutory contribution-month inputs. The database cannot establish several statutory conditions.
- Calculation snapshots save applied rate-rule configurations and review flags. Employer period API exposes entry totals and lines; employee payslips expose earnings, employee deduction and employer contribution lines. The payslip is an output view of saved amounts, not a separate legal calculation.
- `PayrollReviewFlags::critical()` is enforced by the service approval endpoint. Critical flags cannot be waived with a review note. Warning-only flags require a note. Statutory-enabled regular runs receive `unsupported_payroll_rule_requires_review`; tax-enabled employees receive `missing_statutory_input_requires_review`. Monthly salary calendar-day proration also blocks. The annual 13th-month path flags missing statutory data for every positive benefit. Payment recording requires approved/partially-paid period status and enforces remaining balance; it does not make an unsafe calculation valid.
- This is an important safety gate, but it is not a substitute for implementation correctness: keep it in place until relevant calculations and required input validation are verified. Do not switch statutory flags to warnings merely to allow approval.

## Missing data and decisions

1. **Employee coverage/classification:** statutory membership and coverage by program; MWE status and applicable regional wage order; rank-and-file/managerial/field personnel and establishment category/employee count; employment category and work location.
2. **Compensation basis:** full legally relevant compensation definitions for contribution bases; SSS MPF component; multiple employment or previous employer data; guaranteed/basic, allowances, commissions, piece/task, variable and supplementary compensation; rate histories with validated non-overlapping effective ranges.
3. **Tax profile/annual ledger:** employee tax classification, region and current wage order, MWE qualification, prior-employer Form 2316 values, YTD taxable regular/supplementary compensation and withholding, 13th-month and other-benefit aggregate against PHP 90,000, de minimis type/amount/limits, fringe benefits, tax-exempt separation/retirement conditions, annualization/adjustment rules.
4. **Work-time policy/data:** approved daily schedule and workday boundary, rest-day assignment, complete time records, paid/unpaid breaks, overtime authorization and actual OT hours, night hours, holiday/local holiday calendar and applicable status, work suspension, leave type/status/balance and treatment.
5. **Employment lifecycle:** hire and separation dates/reasons, final wages and approved adjustments, unpaid OT/commissions, prorated 13th month, leave conversion, separation/retirement benefit basis, tax refund/withholding, due date, payment and audit evidence.
6. **Company policy:** more favorable benefit/holiday/OT rules, CBA/employment contract terms, payroll frequency versus statutory monthly contribution/remittance treatment, monthly-paid/daily-paid classification, salary proration, rounding and correction workflow. Store policy versions and approvals rather than relying on undocumented operator assumptions.

## Prioritized implementation and regression work

### P0 — maintain safe approval blocks and resolve concrete defects

- Keep statutory-enabled approval blocked until a qualified reviewer signs off the actual target-period rules and calculation fixtures. Add a period-level source verification/effective-date gate rather than relying only on employee-level flag behavior.
- Implement SSS using the full authoritative contribution schedule, including Regular SS, EC and MPF, with separate employee/employer components and eligibility; test every bracket boundary and contribution-table output against the SSS table.
- Correct annual 13th-month tax ceiling to account for aggregate qualifying benefits already paid/accrued (including prior employer inputs when applicable). Test 89,999.99, 90,000.00, 90,000.01 and mixed 13th-month/de minimis/other-benefit cases.
- Verify the exact current PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG effective schedules with the agencies for the target dates; use versioned rules with valid-from/to; do not treat missing schedule as a zero or a warning.
- Validate statutory base/collection/proration for monthly and submonthly payroll with the relevant agencies/accountant; do not prorate by calendar days without authority. Add split-period invariance and monthly remittance reconciliation tests.
- For positive 13th-month payments, derive the eligible annual basic salary and separated employees' proportions from authoritative eligible earnings/periods; block absent/ambiguous inputs.

### P1 — labor standards calculation or mandatory review controls

- Add employee/workplace coverage fields, regional minimum wage schedules, calendar/workday boundaries, rest days, holidays, leave and time-break records, OT approval/hours, night hours, compensation components and policy versions.
- Compute overtime, night differential, rest-day and regular/special-holiday premiums as distinct auditable lines with overlapping-premium rules approved by a labor professional. Add tests for midnight-spanning shifts, holiday/rest-day coincidences, OT beyond eight hours, coverage exemptions and workweek schedule variations.
- Add break/time completeness checks. Never infer a standard unpaid break; block approval if a pay-impacting interval lacks the approved break/work record required by policy.
- Keep entry/register/API/payslip/payment reconciliation tests for each new earning/deduction and assert employer contributions never reduce employee net pay.

### P2 — salary lifecycle and final pay

- Add a distinct final-pay case/workflow for terminated employees, with date/reason, covered wages, unused leave, prorated 13th month, statutory/contractual separation or retirement benefits, tax adjustment, review evidence and release deadline.
- Test resignation, just/authorized cause and redundancy scenarios separately; require professional decisions on entitlement, tax treatment, deduction/offset limits, clearance and collective/company-policy interactions.
- Add regional minimum wage, hire/separation proration, salary change at period edge, partial-day/time, absence/leave, year-end annualization and multi-employer transfer regression tests.

## Items requiring qualified professional confirmation

- Whether SSS/EC/MPF membership, compensation base, rounding, contribution month, and timing in this payroll frequency are correct for each employee; the current formula omission of MPF is concrete and must be resolved.
- Which PhilHealth schedule applies to the actual 2026 pay month and how the applicable monthly basic salary base and employer/employee shares are handled for this employee population and split pay periods.
- Current Pag-IBIG Circular 460 applicability, defined compensation basis, member categories, rate/cap, contribution rounding, and submonthly/fractional-month handling.
- BIR classification for each employee, MWE/region qualification, taxability of every recurring/variable benefit, de minimis limits, shared PHP 90,000 usage, supplemental/year-end tax, prior-employer transfer data, and 13th-month/termination tax.
- DOLE coverage/exemptions and computation for OT, night differential, ordinary rest-day, Sunday, regular/special holiday, coincident days, meal/rest breaks, minimum wage, leave and final pay for the employer's actual region, industry, establishment and workforce.
- Monthly/daily salary proration, payable-day definition and treatment of approved/unpaid absence, maternity/other leave, work suspension, and effective compensation dates.
