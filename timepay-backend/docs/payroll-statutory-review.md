# Payroll calculation review and approval limitations

Reviewed against authoritative government sources accessed 2026-10-27. This is an implementation audit, not legal or accounting advice. A source schedule can be summarized, but employer-specific eligibility and legal correctness require qualified Philippine payroll review.

## Attendance duration and time policy

- `attendance_logs.timestamp` is a database `datetime`; punches are paired by timestamp in application timezone (`Asia/Manila` in the current deployment configuration). Payroll date ranges are inclusive local dates represented as `[start-of-start-date, start-of-day-after-end-date)`. This avoids 23:59:59 clipping and allows adjacent periods to meet without overlapping.
- Completed shift overlap is allocated by elapsed seconds. Hourly wages use exact elapsed seconds multiplied by hourly rate and are rounded to centavos per rate/day segment. Displayed `worked_minutes` are total elapsed seconds rounded once per entry to the nearest minute, half up. This is a display quantity; earnings are calculated from seconds.
- Original punch IDs are linked only to the payroll entry for the period containing each punch. A cross-boundary shift's portions are allocated separately by time; its two punches can therefore belong to separate periods. Approval checks each included source punch and snapshot.
- No break duration, approved overtime amount, attendance correction state, or employee schedule/regular-hours calendar is modeled. A shift beyond configured standard daily hours is included at straight time only in the draft calculation, flagged critically, and blocked from approval. No statutory premium is fabricated.

## Approval gates now enforced

Critical, non-waivable flags include duplicate/overlapping/unverified/incomplete attendance; paid or previously linked attendance; missing/zero effective compensation; long shifts without overtime calculation; disabled overtime eligibility; approved leave needing policy; partial-month salary proration; missing statutory inputs or rules; negative net; and the 13th-month benefit ceiling issue. Critical flags reject approval even with a review note. Warning-only flags require a note. Payments remain available only after approval and cannot exceed saved net pay.

## Not ready for real payroll

The SSS 2025 schedule is verified for its stated effective period, but no newer 2026 schedule was independently confirmed. PhilHealth's cited schedule explicitly covers CY 2025 only. Pag-IBIG's applicable 2026 source/effective schedule remains unverified. BIR calculations lack required employee classifications and annual benefit detail. Overtime, night differential, rest-day/holiday pay, breaks, unpaid leave impacts, and final pay are not calculated. This code is therefore suitable only for synthetic review until a qualified payroll professional verifies effective rules, employer/employee eligibility, and configuration for each period and employee.
